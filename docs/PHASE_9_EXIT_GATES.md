## Gate 3 follow-up (tracked, not blocking)

36 of 44 `FeatureFormatCodec` records carry fidelity metadata only, with no `parse`/`serialize` function, because their real implementations are whole-document walkers rather than single-node functions. Closing this for real — giving every cell a genuine per-node `parse`/`serialize` — requires refactoring `docx/export.ts`, `pdf/format.ts`, and the list/table/block/marks whole-document serializers into a per-node, context-carrying form. That is a substantial new engineering project (explicitly declined as in-scope for this session — see the confirmed decision to wire only the 8 real cells rather than attempt the full refactor), not a continuation of Phase 9 §3's verification work. Flagging it here so it isn't lost, and so a future reader doesn't mistake "gate 3 passes" for "all 44 codecs are fully implemented."
